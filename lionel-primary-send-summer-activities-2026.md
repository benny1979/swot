# Free & Funded Summer Activities for Children with SEND & Their Families
## Local to Lionel Primary School, Brentford (Summer 2026)

This list focuses on **free, funded, or very low-cost** activities, clubs, and events during the summer holidays for children with SEND and their families in Brentford and the wider London Borough of Hounslow.

---

## 1. Holiday Activities and Food (HAF) Programme
**Cost: FREE (for eligible families)**

A government-funded scheme offering free holiday activities **and** a free nutritious meal each day for children aged 5–16 who receive benefits-related free school meals.

**Activities on offer across Hounslow include:**
- Multi-sports (football, basketball, swimming)
- Arts and crafts
- Computer coding
- Circus skills
- VR experience
- High ropes and assault courses
- Day trips (e.g. Hobbledown Heath, Feltham)

**SEND provision:** Children with SEND are actively welcomed. Share your child's needs in advance so providers can put the right support in place.

**How to access:** Your school will send a HAF voucher approximately one month before the holidays. Apply at least 2 weeks in advance. Contact the school office if you haven't received one.

**Contact:** HAF@hounslow.gov.uk

**More info:** [Holiday Activities and Food Programme – Hounslow](https://www.hounslow.gov.uk/schools-colleges/holiday-activities-food-programme)

## 2. Family Hubs SEND-Friendly Summer Fun Day
**Cost: FREE – no booking required, just turn up**

**When:** Thursday 7 August 2026, 12pm–4pm
**Where:** Old Isleworthians Sports Club, Isleworth

**Activities (all free):**
- Bouncy castle
- Baby sensory play
- Petting zoo
- Music and dance sessions
- Multi-sports
- Rock wall climbing

**SEND-specific provision:**
- Dedicated **quiet zone** for children with sensory needs
- Dedicated **SEND activity zone**
- Hounslow's SEND Local Offer team, SENDIASS, and Universal Support team will be on-site all day

**Need support attending?** Email: familyhub@hounslow.gov.uk

**More info:** [Hounslow Family Hubs](https://hounslowconnect.com/services/hounslows-family-hubs)

## 3. SEND Holiday Playschemes (Hounslow Short Breaks)
**Cost: FREE (by referral)**

A holiday playscheme run **specifically for children and young people with SEND and their siblings**, run through Hounslow's Short Breaks service.

- Available to children aged 0–18 with disabilities
- Sessions can be a few hours, half a day, or a full day
- Your child must be referred by Hounslow's social work team

**Contact:** 020 8583 3636

**More info:**
- [SEND Short Breaks – Hounslow](https://www.hounslow.gov.uk/send-local-offer-social-care/send-local-offer-short-breaks)
- [Holiday Playschemes – Hounslow FSD](https://fsd.hounslow.gov.uk/SynergyWeb/Family_Service_Directory/HPS.aspx)

## 4. Hounslow Family Hubs – Drop-In Support (Year-Round)
**Cost: FREE**

**Brentford Family Hub:** TW8 0DJ

## 10. SENDIASS Hounslow (Free Information & Advice)
**Cost: FREE**

SENDIASS (Special Educational Needs and Disabilities Information Advice and Support Service) provides free, impartial advice and support for families of children with SEND. Staff will be present at the Family Hubs Fun Day (see above) and can be reached throughout the summer for guidance on accessing local provision.

Available via: familyhub@hounslow.gov.uk or through Family Hubs
